A procedural exploration game that constructs a new maze layout on every launch. The player must navigate an ever-changing frozen maze, demonstrating algorithmic generation and replayable level structure.

Earth is a Toy

A physics-based alien catching game where animated aliens react to the player's actions. I used Inverse Kinematics (IK) to create procedural character animations, allowing the aliens' bodies and limbs to naturally react to the movement and direction of the gameplay.

Play

Bouchari Neko, my own Mario-style platformer starring a fat cat who speaks in an Osaka dialect. Made fully with AI in Unity: every part of it was built by prompt engineering with Codex.

Play

Olympus Dodge

A first-person fantasy dodgeball game inspired by the feeling of exploring a world like Skyrim. I created the environment background in World Machine and used it to build a large fantasy landscape around the gameplay.

Play

Frog Kamizumo

A 3D reinterpretation of traditional Japanese Kamizumo, transforming the classic paper sumo game into a physical 3D experience. The main technical challenge was creating convincing physics that make the sumo characters react naturally to movement, collisions and impacts.

Play

Underwater Pinball

An old-school pinball game set underwater, developed entirely in Unity. I modeled all of the game's 3D assets myself and programmed the complete gameplay and pinball mechanics.
