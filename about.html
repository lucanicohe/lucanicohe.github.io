Nobody knows my city...

I grew up in Weiden, a medieval town in Bavaria, Germany. It’s a lovely place, but I always wanted to look further and build a future beyond it.

In middle school, a friend showed me Naruto. That one anime started everything. Japanese games came next, and living in Japan, learning from its makers and one day giving something back became my dream.

In 2023, I moved to Japan.

Learning Japanese by learning culture.

After arriving in 2023, I spent two years at Kai Language School, where lessons centered on speaking, and finished in 2025 at Level N2. Today I speak Japanese fluently.

For three years I also worked part time at a restaurant, teaching foreign guests to make sushi. It showed me how Japanese workplaces and their hierarchy really work.

My brother is famous, now it’s my turn.

I enrolled at TECH.C. in 2025 to make the kind of games that raised me.

My brother is a well-known artist. Growing up beside his work gave me an artist’s eye.

So when I make a game, I see it and work on it like an artist.

It shows in the results: wins at school tournaments and hackathons, a corporate project, and the Tokyo Game Show.

19k follow along. One video hit 4M.

While studying in Germany, I worked as a freelance composer and grew my music channel on YouTube. I mostly wrote film music, collaborating with YouTubers on background tracks and with small film studios. My most listened track has reached 2.3 million views on YouTube.

Today I share my game dev journey online. My personal YouTube channel grew to 1k subscribers through two years of weekly uploads. Then, in 2026, I started Instagram, and within just two months it had grown to 3k followers.

What makes me different?

I have two sides. A maker who happily creates for hours, and a mover who trains: regular gym trips, a year of karate up to orange belt, and running.

The two feed each other. Making things gives me ideas and purpose, while sport gives me the energy and a clear head to bring them to life. I try to keep them in balance, a bit like the golden mean: the best place to stand is somewhere in between.

What inspires me

Games

I have played games since I was young, starting on the Nintendo DS. Later, my dad gave me his old work computer, and I explored PC games, beginning with Western ones. Then I found Japanese games, and studios like FromSoftware, SEGA, PlatinumGames and Capcom became the ones I most want to learn from.

D.LEAGUE

An artist friend from Instagram, who works with a company involved in D.LEAGUE, invited me to see it. What hooked me was the companies behind the teams: SEGA (SEGA SAMMY LUX), KADOKAWA (KADOKAWA DREAMS) and CyberAgent (CyberAgent Legit). Their dance teams, their charm. I wish I could join one of them.

Ten years from now.

In 10 years, I see myself leading a team at the company that first took a chance on me: growing its signature work, and the next newcomer.

I see AI not as a threat to my skills, but as one more skill that turns ideas into real work faster.

What AI can’t copy is character. It helped me grow an audience and win events at school, and it stays at the center of everything I build.
