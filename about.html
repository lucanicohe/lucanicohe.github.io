More about me

Who I am, where I come from, and where I’m going.

Origins

From the countryside to the city.

I grew up in Weiden, a medieval town in Bavaria, Germany. It’s a lovely place, but I always wanted to look further and build a future beyond it.

In middle school, a friend recommended Naruto, and that anime started it all. I fell in love with Japanese culture and history, and living in Japan, and one day giving something back, became a dream of mine.

In 2023, I moved to Japan.

Language school

Learning Japanese.

After arriving in 2023, I spent two years at Kai Language School, where lessons centered on speaking, and finished in 2025 at Level N2. Today I speak Japanese fluently.

Living and studying in Japan every day, I’ve come to understand its culture and to take an active part in Japanese society.

TECH.C.

Games with an artist’s eye.

I enrolled at TECH.C. in 2025 to study game programming.

My brother is a well-known artist. Growing up beside his work gave me an artist’s eye.

So when I make a game, I see it and work on it like an artist.

It shows in the results: my games have won many tournaments, hackathons and events at school.

Social media

19k people follow along.

While studying in Germany, I worked as a freelance composer and grew my music channel on YouTube. I mostly wrote film music, collaborating with YouTubers on background tracks and with small film studios. My most listened track has reached 2.3 million views on YouTube.

Today I share my game dev journey online. My personal YouTube channel grew to 1k subscribers through two years of weekly uploads. Then, in 2026, I started Instagram, and within just two months it had grown to 3k followers.

Character

Create. Train. Repeat.

I have two sides. One loves making things and happily spends hours in front of a computer, creating. The other is out and about, staying active: regular trips to the gym, a year of karate up to orange belt, and running.

The two feed each other. Making things gives me ideas and purpose, while sport gives me the energy and a clear head to bring them to life. I try to keep them in balance, a bit like the golden mean: the best place to stand is somewhere in between.

Hobbies

My hobbies.

Games

I have played games since I was young, starting on the Nintendo DS. Later, my dad gave me his old work computer, and I started exploring PC games, beginning with Western games. Over time, I found my way to Japanese games.

D.LEAGUE

An artist friend I know from Instagram, who works with a company involved in D.LEAGUE, invited me and showed it to me. I found it fascinating, especially because of its connection to game companies like SEGA (SEGA SAMMY LUX) and KADOKAWA (KADOKAWA DREAMS).

Future

Building with AI, not against it.

I don’t see AI as a threat to the skills I’ve built. I see it as one more skill, and one that makes it faster to turn ideas into real software.

I have plenty of ideas for the future. I plan to use AI as a tool to bring them to life, and eventually to start a company around them, with the goal of making the world a little better.

What AI can’t copy is character. It’s what has helped me grow an audience and win events at school, and it will stay at the center of everything I build.
